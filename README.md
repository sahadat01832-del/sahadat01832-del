### HerovaAi

I build **HerovaAi** — a self-hosted AI assistant that answers customer questions
on WhatsApp in the owner's own words: your prices, your hours, your rules, plus a
memory of your business. Customers keep using WhatsApp; nothing new to install.

- **What it does:** <https://sahadat01832-del.github.io/herovaai/>
- **Questions answered:** <https://sahadat01832-del.github.io/herovaai/faq/>
- **How it works, and where the app runs:** <https://sahadat01832-del.github.io/>

The assistant is meant for small businesses that cannot staff a phone all day —
shops, bakeries, clinics, repair services — so a question at 21:00 still gets a
useful reply instead of silence.
